About Sunmetal America

Metal components that support safer, better elevator systems.

Sunmetal America serves the elevator industry with a focused range of metal components: troughing, car operating panels, guide rails, small steel fabrications, and decorative panels. We understand that precision matters in both visible finishes and behind-the-scenes hardware.

From structured component supply to tailored fabrication requirements, our approach is grounded in consistent quality, practical engineering, and responsive support for elevator projects.
